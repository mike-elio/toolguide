# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We are committed to providing a welcoming, respectful, and harassment-free environment for everyone who participates in this project, regardless of experience, identity, background, or ability.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, constructive, and specific when discussing ideas or reviewing work.
- Assume good intent while still addressing technical problems directly.
- Respect different perspectives and levels of experience.
- Keep discussions focused on the project and its users.
- Accept responsibility, apologize when appropriate, and learn from mistakes.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, threats, discrimination, or personal attacks.
- Sexualized language, imagery, or unwanted attention.
- Publishing another person's private information without permission.
- Deliberate disruption, spam, or repeated off-topic arguments.
- Any conduct that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional community.

## Reporting

Report conduct concerns privately to the repository owner through the contact options on the owner's GitHub profile. For security vulnerabilities, follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) instead.

Reports will be reviewed in good faith and handled as confidentially as practical. Maintainers may remove content, reject contributions, restrict participation, or take other proportionate action when this code is violated.

## Scope

This code applies in project spaces and when someone is representing the project in public spaces.
